About

Welcome to the Bonneville Beaker, your free community covering all thing science. Keep track of local groundbreaking research with us. It has never been easier. Join us for monthly issues and podcast episodes highlighting the work of scientists that you might not hear otherwise.

We know that scientific research can be hard to understand, dense at best and confusing at worst. Our aim is to build a bridge between the experts and you. We break the details into an easy-to-digest format for a leisurely educational experience.

For those interested, we also provide an array of free educational content on our "Science Made Easy" (SME) page. There you can gain the skills you need to attack the primary literature head-on (also useful for the ACT/GRE).

Creator and Editor

Coleman Nielsen is a life-long science enthusiast. He is recently defended his Ph.D. and hopes to fill his niche in the science industry as a science-communication specialist and educator. His passion for science is only exceeded by his desire to be the world's greatest uncle.

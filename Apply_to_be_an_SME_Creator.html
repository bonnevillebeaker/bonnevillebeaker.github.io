Science Made Easy contributor program

Apply to be an SME Creator

Help build a unified study space where people can bridge the gap between what they understand now and the wider world of science.

Our aim

Make scientific ideas understandable, interactive, and testable.

Science Made Easy brings explanations and hands-on learning into one shared space. An SME contribution should help a learner understand a scientific principle, connect it to the real world, and, when an interactive tool is useful, demonstrate that principle for themselves.

✓

Rules of submission

Who may submit and what is required

All SME submissions must meet every requirement below before they will be reviewed.

  1. Verified graduate-level credentials are required. Applicants must be current graduate students or hold a master's or doctoral degree. Current students must provide an official laboratory webpage. Graduates must provide an electronically accessible thesis or dissertation. Bonneville Beaker may, at its sole discretion, waive this requirement for an applicant who demonstrates equivalent subject-matter expertise; such exceptions are granted rarely and only where clearly warranted.
  2. Independent verification is required. Current graduate students must provide written verification from a verifiable mentor in the relevant scientific community. Applicants who already hold a master's or doctoral degree may instead use a qualified scientific colleague. The verifier must confirm that the finished submission works as described and contains no malicious or concealed behavior.
  3. Only approved, self-contained files are accepted. The ZIP may contain only .html, .css, .js, .txt, .csv, and ordinary static image files. The article and tool must work locally with internet access disabled and may not use external links, APIs, CDNs, remote files, analytics, or tracking.
  4. JavaScript must be licensed under GNU GPL v3.0. Every submitted .js file must be original source code distributed under the GNU General Public License version 3.0. Include LICENSE.txt and identify the license in README.txt.
  5. All material must be original and lawful. AI may be used in code-developement and writing outlining. However, you are ultimately responsible for the final product and we do not endorse the writing of prose in our articles by AI. Do not submit copyrighted, trademarked, plagiarized, or otherwise unauthorized code, text, data, images, or branding.
  6. Acceptance and compensation are not guaranteed. Submission does not promise review, acceptance, publication, continued hosting, payment, royalties, employment, reimbursement, or compensation of any kind. Participation is voluntary and at will.
  7. Bonneville Beaker may propose modifications. Scientific, editorial, accessibility, security, interface, and styling changes may be requested so the contribution fits the site. The submitter will review and approve all modifications before final publication.

What we are looking for

Two kinds of SME contributions

01

Interactive science tools

Create a browser-based JavaScript tool that teaches or demonstrates a scientific principle.

  • The tool must be submitted with a companion article for the SME Topics menu.
  • The article should explain the science, refer directly to the tool, and guide the learner through activities or examples.
  • The learner should be able to change inputs, observe results, and use the tool to demonstrate the principles discussed in the article.
  • The tool should work in a modern browser without requiring installation.
02

Discussion-based topics

Submit an article exploring a high-level scientific concept that would not be meaningfully improved by adding an interactive tool.

  • Build the discussion around a clear scientific question, concept, controversy, or framework.
  • Define technical terms and write for an interested non-specialist audience.
  • Use reliable sources and distinguish established evidence from interpretation or speculation.
  • Include prompts, examples, or thought experiments that encourage active learning.
